Sterling Logistics is an ETS2 virtual trucking company focused on reliable records, meaningful progression and a connected driver experience rather than noise and disposable stats.

What we are building

One Sterling platform.

The goal is simple: a driver should not have separate identities and histories across the tracker, website and Discord. Sterling Tracker reads ETS2 locally, sends appropriate operational data to the backend, and the website and Discord bot consume the same company records.

That means completed jobs are permanent, driver totals can be trusted, and future features such as finances, achievements and DriveScore have one source of truth.

01 · Local first

Fast telemetry stays on the PC

Driving gauges remain responsive because local ETS2 telemetry does not wait on the internet.

02 · Backend truth

Jobs become durable records

Started, delivered and cancelled jobs are tracked through the Sterling backend rather than relying on local history alone.

03 · Connected platform

Website and Discord reflect the same company

Meaningful events and statistics flow outward without turning every telemetry frame into server noise.
