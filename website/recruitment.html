Recruitment

Drive with purpose.
Grow with Sterling.

Recruitment should set clear expectations: respectful driving, genuine activity, reliable telemetry and a willingness to be part of a professional ETS2 community.

01Identity

Discord-connected membership

Your Sterling identity is intended to connect to Discord rather than forcing you to maintain another password and account system.

02Driving

Quality over grinding

Distance matters, but clean deliveries, consistency and safe driving should matter too.

03Community

Show up professionally

Convoys, events and Discord should feel organised without turning Sterling into a second job.

Step 1

Join the Sterling community

The public Discord/recruitment destination will be linked here once the permanent invite is confirmed.

Step 2

Complete recruitment

Staff can review the application and link the accepted driver to a Sterling Driver ID.

Step 3

Install Sterling Tracker

Once approved, the driver gets the clean Windows download experience rather than needing to understand GitHub Actions.

What drivers get

A career, not just a counter.

Jobs, miles, earnings, achievements, DriveScore, convoy attendance and company activity are intended to build a long-term Sterling profile.

The economics model, including driver share, remains configurable so business rules can change without being hard-coded into every part of the platform.
