Do I need thousands of ETS2 hours?

No. We care more about attitude, consistency and whether Sterling is a good fit for you.

Is TruckersMP required?

A TruckersMP identity is requested because Sterling convoys and public events may use TruckersMP.

What happens after I apply?

Your application enters the recruitment queue. Staff review it and contact you through Discord if further information is needed.

Will my draft disappear?

No. While you type, the form stores a draft in your browser until you submit it or press Clear draft.

Do I need Sterling Tracker immediately?

No. Tracker onboarding comes after acceptance so new applicants do not need to install anything before joining.

Can I apply again if declined?

Yes, unless staff tell you otherwise. Recruitment can explain what would need to change before a future application.
